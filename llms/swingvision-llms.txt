# SwingVision

> SwingVision is an AI-powered tennis and pickleball platform built by A.I. experts from Tesla and Apple, venture-backed by Tennis Australia, Sony, and Techstars. Its mobile app processes video on-device with computer vision to deliver automated line calling, scoring, match stats, and highlight reels from a single phone or tablet camera.

SwingVision is a consumer application. It does not publish a public developer API, API documentation, or SDKs; its api.swing.vision backend is private and undocumented. Session data can be exported from the app as video clips and spreadsheet (Excel) stats files.
